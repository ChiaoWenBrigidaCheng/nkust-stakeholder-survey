National Kaohsiung University of Science and Technology (NKUST) is committed to sustainable development and continues to integrate environmental, social, and governance (ESG) principles into university management and development strategies. To better understand stakeholders’ concerns and expectations regarding the University’s material sustainability topics, we sincerely invite you to complete this questionnaire.

This survey is anonymous. Your responses will be used solely for materiality analysis and the preparation of the University’s sustainability report. The survey takes approximately 3–5 minutes to complete.
